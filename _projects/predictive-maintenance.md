End-to-end sensor time-series ML system: sliding-window DSP features, XGBoost RUL (RMSE 16.80 on NASA CMAPSS) and bearing-fault detection (PR-AUC 0.9998 on CWRU), served via FastAPI with MLflow tracking, Docker, and CI/CD on Azure Container Apps.
